My Love for Delicious Food

My mom always told me when I was a child that I had a strong love and attraction for food. As I grew up, I understood why she said that. I discovered that I truly love food, enjoy cooking, and love exploring new food cultures around the world. For me, food is essential to human life. Without good food, we lose energy, strength, and joy. Eating well helps us feel better, stay active, and succeed in our daily lives. Every meal can tell a story about people, traditions, and places we have never visited before
